# Talk to the Wrist

Sometimes people ask you things and you just... don't want to deal with it. Now your Pebble can deal with it for you.

## What it does

Four big buttons. Four responses. Zero effort.

| Button | Says |
|--------|------|
| YES | "Yes." |
| NO | "No." |
| SHUT UP | "Shut up." |
| GET OUTTA HERE | "Get outta here." |

Tap a button, your watch says the thing, conversation over.

## Settings

Long-press the back button to open settings. Five volume levels from "politely dismissive" to "absolutely not."

## Requirements

- Pebble Time 2 (emery platform)
- Situations that require a response but not a conversation
